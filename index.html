About

Three sides of the same economics

Consultant, airline, airport. The same questions look different from each chair.

At CAPA I advised airport operators on privatisation bids and AERA tariff determinations, and airlines on network, fleet and cost. At flynas I was inside the airline, co-leading the WINGS initiatives portfolio and taking 8% out of non-fuel CASK. At Riyadh Airports I run the strategy lifecycle for King Khalid International, from formulation through Matarat approval to execution.

The thread is transformation: finding where value actually sits, then building the case that moves capital toward it.

Before any of it, business journalism at Reuters, covering oil and commodity markets. That is where writing clearly about numbers-heavy subjects started.
