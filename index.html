About

Three sides of the same economics

A landing charge is revenue on one side of the table and a cost line on the other. I have sat on both sides, and advised from outside them.

At CAPA that meant privatisation bids and AERA tariff cases for airport operators, and network, fleet and cost plans for airlines. flynas put me inside the airline, where I took 8% out of non-fuel CASK and learned how little of a cost base is actually escapable. Riyadh Airports put me back on the infrastructure side, running the strategy lifecycle for King Khalid International from formulation through Matarat approval to execution.

Before any of it, Reuters, on oil and commodities. Nothing sharpens an argument like filing into a market that has already moved.
