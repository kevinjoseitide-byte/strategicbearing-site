About

Clarity of direction in complex environments

A true bearing tells you where you actually stand, versus where you meant to go.

Kevin is an aviation strategist with experience spanning airlines, airports and consulting. Over the past decade he has led and supported work across corporate strategy, business planning, cost transformation and investment evaluation — for airport operators facing tariff determinations, for airlines restructuring their cost base, and for boards deciding where capital should go.

He holds a Master’s in International Management from Trinity College Dublin, and began in business journalism at Reuters, covering oil and commodity markets. That is where the discipline of writing clearly about numbers-heavy subjects started.
