Route feasibility

Does this route earn its keep?

Size the market, take a share of it by cabin, price it at a yield, fly it with a given aircraft and frequency. Cost splits two ways: escapable if you don't operate, carried either way if you do. Costs normalise per block hour or per departure depending on what actually drives them.

Market and capture

O&D pairMarket, pax/yrGrowth % Business %Capture, Y1 econ %Capture, Y5 econ %

Market is total one-way passenger journeys, both directions summed, as O&D data reports it. Capture ramps straight-line from Y1 to Y5. Business-cabin capture is taken as 0.8× the economy rate unless the market is business-heavy.

Service

Yields are round-trip fares net of tax. The model halves them to value a one-way journey, so the market figure and the fare stay on the same footing.

Fuel, maintenance and crew accrue with time in the air. Lease, insurance and the engineering and station overheads are carried whether or not this route operates, so they are not escapable by cancelling it.

Charged per turn, not per hour. A departure here is one leg, so a round trip incurs two.

Five-year view

How to read this. Cash breakeven asks whether the route covers the cost you would avoid by not flying it. Full breakeven asks whether it also covers the cost you carry anyway. A route can clear cash and still destroy value, which is the case most often argued about in a network meeting. Figures are indicative and no better than the assumptions typed above; this is a structure for the argument, not a substitute for it.
