Each airport is priced at the period in force today, on the inputs above. This is a total-cost comparison, not a like-for-like rate comparison: the airports levy different lines, so Delhi carries X-ray baggage charges, Bengaluru and Hyderabad carry CUTE/CUSS/BRS, and Hyderabad alone carries bridge-mounted equipment and the fuel infrastructure charge. Aircraft charges are landing and parking; passenger charges are UDF and per-passenger terminal charges.

Source and standing. Rates are transcribed from the AERA 4th Control Period final tariff orders, checked against the page images. Nothing is interpolated.

What this does not include, and what to check.

  • Tariffs are ceiling rates and exclude GST. The Aviation Security Fee is set by the Ministry of Civil Aviation separately and is not here. Nor is ground handling, fuel throughput beyond the Hyderabad infrastructure charge, or the Mumbai Development Fee levied under Order 09/2023-24.
  • Variable Tariff Plans and the Key Partnership Programme are narrative and conditional in the orders rather than tabulated, so they are not modelled. They can move landing charges materially on a qualifying new international route — Delhi and Mumbai both show nil landing for a qualifying new long-haul destination in its first year.
  • Appeals are live. TDSAT partly set aside Order 20/2024-25 on DIAL's appeal, and on 29.05.2026 allowed MIAL's appeal and directed AERA to give effect within three months. No revised Delhi or Mumbai rate card was on the AERA tariff-orders list as at 29.10.2026.
  • Bengaluru and Hyderabad contingent add-ons apply only once AERA issues an Addendum on project commissioning. They are off by default and labelled where they appear.
  • Bengaluru parking is given as two rows — the first two hours after the free period, and beyond four hours — with no row for the two to four hour band. This tool reads the slabs against total time on stand: free for two hours, the first rate for hours two to four, the second beyond four. That closes the gap in the order rather than reproducing it.
  • Rates are what the orders print. Concessions, exemptions, general conditions and any carrier-specific arrangement sit outside this tool.

A working model, not advice. Check the order before you price anything on it.
