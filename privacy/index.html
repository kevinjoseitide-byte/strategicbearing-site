Privacy

What happens to your information when you visit this site.

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit strategicbearing.in. The site is run by Kevin Jose. If you have a question, email josek@tcd.ie.

The short version

You can read everything here without an account. There are no sign-up forms and no comments. The site runs no analytics and sets no cookies of its own. Where ads appear, they are served by Google, which uses cookies to show them; where the law requires it, you are asked for your choice first.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google’s Ads Settings. You can opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or, in Europe, at youronlinechoices.eu. Google explains what it collects from sites that use its services in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and its own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform asks for your choice before ads use cookies or personal data. You can change that choice at any time through the privacy and cookie settings link the message provides. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads.

Analytics

This site uses no analytics or tracking tool of its own — not Google Analytics, not any other. If that ever changes, this page will be updated before it does.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted by Netlify, which processes standard request data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested in order to serve and secure the site. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it fetches them.

Tools

The calculators under Tools run entirely in your browser. What you type into them is never sent to this site or anywhere else.

Email and links

If you email me, I use your message and your address to reply and for nothing else, and I do not share them. Links out to other sites, such as LinkedIn and Substack, are governed by those sites’ own privacy policies.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. This site holds none, beyond any email you choose to send. For data Google holds about you, use your Google account settings or the links above. You can also write to josek@tcd.ie.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
