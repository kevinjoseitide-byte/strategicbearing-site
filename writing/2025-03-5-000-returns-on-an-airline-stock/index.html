Capital 11 March 2025

5,000% returns on an airline stock?

When we think of multibaggers, airlines rarely come to mind. However, Air Canada (AC.TO) defied the odds & transformed itself from near bankruptcy to a benchmark of shareholder value creation delivering a staggering 5,000%+ return for investors in just eight years.

📈 From Crisis to Unprecedented Growth
>In 2012, AC traded at less than CAD 1. By 2019, its stock hit an all-time high of CAD 50, marking an incredible 5,000%+ growth.
>A CAD 20,000 investment in 2012 would have grown to over CAD 1M by 2020.

📌 Strategic Levers Behind Air Canada’s Exceptional Turnaround

💰 1. Revenue Enhancement Initiatives
2030 Revenue Goal: Surpass CAD 30 billion (7–8% annual growth).
Key Drivers:
1. Expansion in the U.S. 6th Freedom market (connecting global traffic through Canadian hubs.)
2. Premium cabin growth & expanded long-haul international routes.
3. Revamped Aeroplan

🛠️ 2. Cost Discipline & Margin Expansion
>Targeting EBITDA margins of 18–20% by 2030 (300+ bps improvement).
1. Modernizing fleet with fuel-efficient aircraft (15-20% lower fuel & MX costs).
2. Smarter crew scheduling, predictive maintenance & automation leveraging AI.

✈️ 3. Fleet & Network Optimization
1. Fleet optimization/renewal, adding Boeing 787-10s, A321XLRs, and A220s.
2. 737 MAX ops by Air Canada Rouge, targeting leisure markets.
3. Focused international network growth targeting markets with strong demographic growth (India, APAC, and Europe).

🎖️ 4. Operational Excellence & CX
1. Significant improvement in OTP
2. Customer-centric initiatives to increase NPS by 12 points by 2028.

💳 5. Loyalty & Ancillary Revenue
1. Aeroplan loyalty membership doubled among youngsters.
2. 50% growth in co-branded credit card spending post-relaunch.
3. Expanding ancillary revenue streams and partnerships.

📲 6. Digital Transformation & Innovation
1. Significant investment in digital capabilities: AI-driven customer platforms, dynamic pricing models, and enhanced digital experiences.
2. Free and fast WiFi fleet-wide by 2026.

📊 7. Financial Discipline & Capital Allocation
1. Maintaining liquidity levels above 15% of revenue, targeting a conservative debt leverage ratio (<2x EBITDA by 2028).
2. CAD 4–5 billion cumulative free cash flow generation (2024–2028).
3. Share buybacks & reinvestments to drive sustained value creation.

👥 8. People & Performance Culture
Major focus on employee engagement and retention.

>Gains for IPO investors weren't as significant.
>COVID heavily impacted ops and stock price dropped to a low of CAD 9 in 2020.

However, a strong recovery is underway, underpinned by low valuation (attractive low P/E multiples) and continued fundamental strength.

While airlines are generally considered capital traps, AC proved that long-term value creation is possible.
