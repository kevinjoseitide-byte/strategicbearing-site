Flightpath 4 February 2025

700 aircraft for 1.4 Bn people

700 aircraft for 1.4 Bn people. India’s Aviation Growth is Just Getting Started! ✈️🇮🇳

🔹 Largest Population, Smallest Fleet
India (1.44B) has just 700 commercial passenger aircraft, compared to China’s 3,750 and the US’s 4,850 (excludes regional aircraft for accurate benchmarking)

🔹 A Decade of Massive Growth
In the last odd 10 years:
✅ Airports doubled: 74 → 157
✅ Passenger traffic doubled: 110M → 220M annually
✅ Fleet doubled: ~350 → ~700

📈 From 2009-2019, India's air traffic grew at 2x the rate of GDP growth

🔹 A Market Ripe for Expansion
📉 Aircraft per million people: India (0.5) vs. China (2.7) (-81%) vs. US (14.4) (-97%)
📉 Domestic seats per capita: India (0.13) vs. China (0.59) (-78%) vs. US (3.11) (-96%)

🔹 A Sleeping Giant in International Traffic
🌍 Only 7% of Indians hold passports → A massive untapped outbound travel market
🌍 India has the world’s largest diaspora, fueling global air travel demand
🌍 Burgeoning trade & business travel signal a surge in air travel demand
🌍 India’s international seat capacity is ~50% of China’s with room for exponential growth

🔹 Growing Disposable Income
💰 Union Budget Tax Exemption: No personal income tax for individuals earning under ₹12,75,000 (~$15,300) → More spending power for travel
💰 A booming middle class & rising leisure travel is fueling demand
💰 Shift from Rail to Air: As incomes rise, a significant shift from long-distance rail to air travel is expected. Indian Railways carried 6.7 billion pax in FY24.

🔹 China’s Growth as a Benchmark
📈 China’s aviation boom accelerated when GDP (PPP) per capita hit $10K-$12K. India is now at $11K and poised for a similar growth trajectory (provided there’s policy support) - this is a challengable assumption and is merely directional

🔹 What’s Next for India’s Aviation Growth?
Fleet Growth | Airport & Infrastructure Expansion | Regional Connectivity | Investment & Policy Support

India’s aviation boom is INEVITABLE!

📌 Regional Fleet Comparison
✈️ US: ~1,800 regional aircraft
✈️ China: ~270 regional aircraft
✈️ India: ~100 regional aircraft

📊 Sources: FAA, CAAC, DGCA, IMF others
(All data in the table corresponds to 2023/24)
(Aircraft numbers are rounded off and directional at best)
